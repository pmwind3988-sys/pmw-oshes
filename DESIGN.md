## Brand & Style

The design system is the Google Stitch `Industrial Logic` system from the `SharePoint Multi-Form Platform` project. It is a high-utility enterprise product style for multi-form operational workflows, with dense layouts, clear hierarchy, Microsoft 365 familiarity, and an industrial corporate tone.

## Layout & Spacing

Use a 64px fixed top navigation bar on authenticated screens and a 240px side navigation rail on desktop. Content uses 24px desktop margins, 16px gutters, and a strict 4px spacing baseline. Mobile collapses into a single-column page with bottom navigation.

## Components

Use dense data tables with 32px-40px rows, white or near-white panel surfaces, low-contrast outlines, compact status chips, square-ish 4px controls, and Inter for all UI text. Buttons and inputs use 4px radius; structural cards use 8px radius. Shadows are reserved for floating or major surfaces only.
